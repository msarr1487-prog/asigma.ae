Core Business

Finance Automation
& Business Intelligence

Reporting automation is not one service among others at ASIGMA — it is our core business. We transform how organisations produce, control, and exploit their financial data: faster closes, live dashboards, regulatory outputs without manual effort.

Most finance functions still run on fragmented spreadsheets, manual consolidations, and month-end processes that consume weeks of senior resource. The data exists — the problem is its architecture.

ASIGMA operates at the intersection of financial expertise and technology. We design reporting systems that are audit-ready, scalable, and genuinely useful to decision-makers — from a standalone Power BI dashboard to a full ERP migration with automated regulatory outputs.

📡

Why now? The convergence of cloud ERP platforms, AI-assisted analytics, and tightening regulatory timelines has made finance automation a board-level priority. Firms that invest now gain a structural cost and speed advantage that compounds over time.

Power BI Financial Dashboards

Real-time P&L, balance sheet, and KPI monitoring — replacing static monthly reports with live, interactive views for management and boards.

Automated Financial Close

Reducing month-end close cycles through workflow automation, reconciliation scripts, and structured data flows between systems.

ERP Migration & Integration

Structured transitions to modern ERP platforms with financial data integrity guaranteed throughout the process.

Regulatory Reporting Automation

Automated production of IFRS and Solvency II outputs — reducing manual intervention in high-stakes regulatory submissions.

AI-Assisted Analysis

Variance analysis, forecasting, and anomaly detection augmented by AI — freeing senior staff for interpretation, not data assembly.
